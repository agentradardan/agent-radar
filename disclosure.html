How Agent Radar makes money, and how we keep it honest.

Disclosure

Agent Radar is free to use. To cover costs, we may earn money in two ways. Both are always disclosed.

Affiliate links

Some “Visit” and “Official site” links are affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These links use rel="sponsored", and tool pages with an affiliate link show a note next to the button.

Sponsored listings

Tool makers can pay for a featured spot: the top of their category and a card in the homepage Featured strip for a fixed period. Featured listings are always marked with a “Sponsored” badge. Details on advertising.

What money doesn't change

Pricing summaries and free-tier info come from each tool's official site, whether or not the tool is a sponsor or has an affiliate program. Sponsors can't edit their pricing data or buy a better free-tier badge. Pricing changes often, so always confirm on the official pricing page.
