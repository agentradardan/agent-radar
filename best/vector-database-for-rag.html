Best vector database for RAG

Updated October 25, 2026. Prices and limits are as of Oct 2026, taken from each vendor's official pricing page (linked below). Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Summary

There is no single best vector database for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG); the right one depends on whether you want to run servers, how much data you have and how much you want to spend. All four here have a free option as of Oct 2026. Pinecone is fully managed with a free Starter plan and paid plans from $20/month. Qdrant and Weaviate are open source with managed clouds that each have a free tier. Chroma is open source and its cloud bills purely by usage after a $0 Starter plan. In our view, Chroma is the quickest way to prototype, Pinecone is the simplest if you never want to manage infrastructure, and Qdrant or Weaviate are strong picks if you want the option to self-host later. Which should you pick?

  • Pick Chroma if you're prototyping a RAG app and want something you can run locally in minutes.
  • Pick Pinecone if you want a hosted service and don't want to think about servers, clusters or scaling.
  • Pick Qdrant if you want open source with a clear path to managed, hybrid or private cloud deployments.
  • Pick Weaviate if hybrid search, multi-tenancy or built-in embeddings matter for your app.
  • In our view, all four handle typical RAG workloads, so try your own data on two free tiers before you commit.

FAQ

Which vector database is free?

All four have a free option as of Oct 2026: Pinecone's Starter plan, Qdrant's Free tier, Weaviate's Free plan and Chroma's $0 Starter plan (plus usage, with free credits). Qdrant, Weaviate and Chroma can also be self-hosted. Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Which is best for RAG in production?

In our view, any of the four can serve production RAG. Pinecone, Qdrant, Weaviate and Chroma each list uptime or support SLAs on at least some paid tiers. Choose based on whether you want fully managed or self-hostable, and on your data size.

Can I self-host?

Qdrant, Weaviate and Chroma are open source and can be self-hosted. Pinecone offers a Bring Your Own Cloud option that runs Pinecone in your cloud account, which is a managed service rather than self-hosting.

What's the cheapest way to start?

Use a free tier. As of Oct 2026, Pinecone's Starter includes up to 2 GB storage, Qdrant's Free tier includes 4 GB disk, Weaviate's Free plan allows 100,000 objects, and Chroma's Starter plan includes $5 in free credits.
