Lovable vs Bolt

Updated November 2, 2026. Prices and limits are as of Nov 2026, taken from each vendor's official pricing page (linked below). Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Summary

Lovable and Bolt both build web apps from a chat prompt in the browser and both have free plans, but they meter usage differently. As of Nov 2026, Lovable's free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month) plus 20 Cloud credits a month, and its paid plans are priced by shared workspace credits rather than seats. Bolt's free plan gives 1M tokens a month with a 300K daily limit, Pro is $25/month with 10M tokens and rollover, and Teams is $30/month per member. In our view, Lovable suits non-technical builders and teams who want one shared credit pool, while Bolt suits people who like a token allowance they can see and carry over, and who may want to pick their own database provider. Which should you pick?

  • Pick Lovable if you're non-technical, want the simplest chat-first experience, or need many teammates on one plan.
  • Pick Bolt if you want a larger free allowance to experiment with, token rollover on Pro, or your own choice of database provider.
  • In our view, build the same small app in both free plans and compare the result and the usage it took before paying.

FAQ

Which is cheaper, Lovable or Bolt?

It depends on usage. As of Nov 2026, Bolt Pro is $25/month for 10M tokens. Lovable's paid plans are priced by credits, and we couldn't verify their prices on its pricing page today, so check lovable.dev/pricing for current pricing.

Do both have a free plan?

Yes. As of Nov 2026, Lovable's free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month) plus 20 Cloud credits a month, and Bolt's free plan gives 1M tokens a month with a 300K daily limit.

Can I use a custom domain?

On Bolt, custom domains start at Pro; the free plan shows Bolt branding. Check Lovable's pricing page for which of its plans include custom domains.

Who owns the code?

Lovable's pricing FAQ says you own the code and projects you build. Bolt's pricing page doesn't address ownership, so check its terms.
