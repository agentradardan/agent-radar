Lovable vs Base44 vs Replit

Updated October 25, 2026. Prices and limits are as of Oct 2026, taken from each vendor's official pricing page (linked below). Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Summary

Lovable, Base44 and Replit all turn a chat prompt into a working web app, and all three have a free plan as of Oct 2026. Lovable gives 5 build credits a day on its free plan and prices paid plans by credits rather than seats. Base44 includes a backend, database and user auth on every plan, with paid plans from $16/month billed annually. Replit runs a full browser-based development environment with hosting, with Core at $20/month ($18 billed annually). In our view, Lovable and Base44 suit people who want to stay in chat and avoid code, while Replit suits people who expect to look at and edit the code themselves. Which should you pick?

  • Pick Lovable if you want to build websites and web apps purely by chat, especially as a team sharing one credit balance.
  • Pick Base44 if your app needs sign-in, a database and integrations from day one and you'd rather not set them up yourself.
  • Pick Replit if you want to see and edit the code, or plan to grow the project into a fuller software build.
  • In our view, build the same small app on each free plan first; the free tiers are enough to see which workflow you prefer.

FAQ

Which has the most generous free plan?

They measure usage differently. As of Oct 2026, Lovable's free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month), Base44's gives 25 message credits a month for up to 5 apps, and Replit's Starter plan gives daily Agent credits up to a monthly cap plus one published app. Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Do I own the code?

Lovable and Base44 both state on their pricing pages that you own what you build, and Base44 describes two-way GitHub sync for exporting source code. Replit projects are code you can open and edit in the workspace.

Which is best for apps with user accounts and a database?

In our view Base44 makes this easiest, since its pricing page lists authentication and database features on every plan. Replit includes production databases among its cloud credits, and Lovable apps run on its Cloud with an included monthly grant.

Do any of them charge per seat?

Lovable says it doesn't charge per seat; workspaces have unlimited members sharing credits. Base44 and Replit price by plan; Replit's Pro plan supports up to 15 collaborators.
