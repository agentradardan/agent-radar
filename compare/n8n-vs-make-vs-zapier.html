n8n vs Make vs Zapier

Updated October 25, 2026. Prices and limits are as of Oct 2026, taken from each vendor's official pricing page (linked below). Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Summary

All three build automated workflows between apps, but they bill differently. n8n charges per full workflow run (an execution) no matter how many steps it has, and offers a free self-hosted Community Edition. Make bills in credits, with most actions using one credit and 1,000 free credits a month. Zapier bills in tasks, with each successful step using a task, and its Free plan gives 100 tasks a month for two-step Zaps. In our view, n8n suits technical users who want self-hosting or long multi-step workflows, Make suits people who like a visual canvas and a usable free plan, and Zapier suits those who want the widest choice of ready-made app connections with the least setup. Which should you pick?

  • Pick n8n if you are comfortable with a technical tool and want self-hosting, or your workflows have many steps and you'd rather pay per run.
  • Pick Make if you want a visual builder and a free plan with enough monthly credits to run real scenarios.
  • Pick Zapier if you need a specific app integration that the others lack, or want the simplest setup for a non-technical team.
  • In our view, estimate how many steps and runs your main workflow needs per month, then price it on each vendor's pricing page before deciding.

FAQ

Is n8n free?

The self-hosted Community Edition is available on GitHub at no cost. As of Oct 2026, n8n's cloud plans start at €20/month billed annually, with a free trial. Check the vendor's site for current pricing.

Which has the best free plan?

It depends on your workflow. As of Oct 2026, Make's Free plan includes 1,000 credits a month, and Zapier's Free plan includes 100 tasks a month for two-step Zaps. n8n's free option is self-hosting. In our view Make's free plan is the most generous for multi-step workflows.

How do executions, credits and tasks differ?

n8n counts one execution per full workflow run. Make counts credits per module action, with most actions using one credit. Zapier counts a task for each successful action step, while triggers and its built-in tools don't count.

Can I self-host Make or Zapier?

Neither pricing page describes a self-hosted option. n8n offers self-hosting through its Community Edition and its Business plan.
