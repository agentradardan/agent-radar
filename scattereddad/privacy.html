Privacy

What I collect, and what I don't

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Scattered Dad (scattereddad.com) is run by Dan, a sole proprietor in Florida, USA. I collect as little as possible. You can read everything here without an account.

What I collect

  • Newsletter: if you sign up, your email address. That's all the form asks for.
  • Emails you send me: I keep them so I can reply.
  • Server logs: my host, Vercel, processes standard technical data (like IP address, browser type and pages requested) to deliver and secure the site.
  • Analytics: Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies and reports aggregated page views.

No advertising trackers. No tracking cookies.

How it's used

Your email is used only to send the Scattered Dad newsletter. It's kept on its own list, separate from any other project.

Who processes it

  • Vercel: hosting, server logs and Web Analytics
  • Resend: storing the newsletter list and sending emails

I don't sell your data

I don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and I don't share it except with the providers above or when the law requires it.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link. To have your email deleted completely, or to ask what I hold about you, email agentradar.dan@gmail.com.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child gave me their email, contact me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date above.

Not medical, mental-health or nutrition advice. This is one dad's personal experience. Talk to a qualified professional about ADHD care or weight management. Some links may earn me a commission someday; if they do, they'll be labeled right next to the link.
