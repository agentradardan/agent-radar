Grok Bot

Grok Bot setups for a scattered brain

Copy-paste setups that carry some of the remembering for me. Life systems, not treatment.

TL;DR
  • Grok Bot is an AI assistant that can chat, take voice notes, connect apps like Gmail and Google Calendar, and run scheduled routines.
  • Below: 5 setups I'm starting with. Copy one, swap the [BRACKETS], send it.
  • The Bot drafts and reminds. Anything that changes your calendar or sends email waits for your OK.
  • I use Grok Bot to help run this site. Nobody paid for this page, and there are no paid links on it.

Why bother

A lot of my ADHD friction is remembering. The routine. The appointment. The thing I said I'd do. An assistant that messages me on a schedule carries some of that load, so my brain doesn't have to.

These are the setups I'm starting with. I'll report back on video about which ones stick and which ones I mute by Thursday.

What it can do

  • Chat by text, voice note or voice call, on desktop, mobile, Slack or X.
  • Connect apps you already use, like Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion and Google Drive.
  • Turn something you ask for often into a routine on a schedule.
  • Remember your preferences, so you don't re-explain your setup.
  • Draft emails and messages for you to approve and send.

Source: Grok Bot overview (official docs) (opens in a new tab), checked November 9, 2026. Features and plans can change.

The five setups

Replace anything in [BRACKETS] with your details. For the scheduled ones, ask the Bot to make it a routine.

Setup 1

Morning checklist

Same checklist, same time, every weekday. The routine stops being something I have to remember.

Every weekday at 7:00 AM, send me my morning checklist: [KEYS, WALLET, PHONE CHARGER; ANYTHING ELSE]. Then list today's calendar events with the time I need to leave for each. End by asking me which one thing matters most today. Keep it under 10 lines.

Tip: Ask the Bot to make it a routine, then check the saved schedule and your time zone.

Setup 2

Brain dump sorter

Dump everything in one messy message (voice note works). Get back a short, sorted list.

Here is my brain dump: [EVERYTHING ON YOUR MIND, IN ANY ORDER]. Sort it into Today, This week, Later and Not mine. For each Today item, give me one tiny first step that takes under 5 minutes. Don't add anything I didn't say. Keep it short.

Tip: If you've connected a to-do app like Todoist or Notion, you can ask it to add the Today items there.

Setup 3

Sunday review

A Sunday look at the week, so Monday doesn't ambush me.

Every Sunday at 6:00 PM, read my Google Calendar for the coming week. Flag clashes, packed days and early starts. List anything I told you I would do this week. Then send me a short plan for Monday with no more than 3 priorities. Don't create, move or cancel any event without asking me.

Tip: Needs Google Calendar connected. With two households, this is where pickup clashes show up early.

Setup 4

Appointment prep

The night before: what's tomorrow, when to leave and what to bring.

Every evening at 8:00 PM, look at tomorrow's calendar. For each appointment, tell me the time, when I need to leave, what to bring (paperwork, ID, payment) and any questions I wrote down for it. If something has no address or details, flag it. Don't change any event.

Tip: Keep sensitive details out of reminder text if other people can see your phone.

Setup 5

Work-block check-ins

Short check-ins during a work block, so I notice when I've drifted.

Today from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM, check in with me every 30 minutes. Ask in one line what I'm working on and whether I'm still on it. If I say I drifted, ask me for the next tiny step and nothing else. Stop at 3:00 PM.

Tip: These are messages from an AI, not a person. If you want a real person working alongside you, see Focusmate on the tools page.

House rules for any AI assistant

  • Start with one setup. Add another only after the first one sticks for a week or two.
  • Keep "ask me before sending, booking or changing anything" in your instructions.
  • Double-check important dates and times. AI makes mistakes.
  • Don't paste health records, passwords or account numbers into any chat.
  • It can remind and organize. It isn't a doctor, coach or therapist.

Not medical advice. Grok Bot isn't a clinician and doesn't diagnose or treat ADHD. Other assistants with reminders or scheduled tasks can run similar setups.
