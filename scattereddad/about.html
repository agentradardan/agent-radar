About

Hi. I'm Dan.

Divorced dad, ADHD brain, 48 years in. This is where I figure it out out loud.

TL;DR
  • 48, divorced, ADHD, twin daughters heading to college in fall 2027.
  • I'm starting an on-camera channel about all of it, including my weight loss.
  • This site is the home base: videos, tools that help me focus, and Grok Bot setups.
  • I'm not a doctor, therapist or dietitian. Nothing here is medical advice.

The short story

I have ADHD. I'm divorced. My twin daughters head off to college in fall 2027, which is coming up faster than my brain is comfortable with.

For a long time my "system" was remembering things. That works great until it doesn't. Missed forms. Forgotten calls. A car inspection sticker from a different era.

So I'm rebuilding. Systems instead of willpower. Tools instead of guilt. And I'm doing it on camera, because if I'm going to lose the keys anyway, I might as well get a video out of it.

What the channel covers

  • ADHD, day to day. The tools, routines and workarounds I try, and which ones survive past week two.
  • Divorced-dad life. Logistics, two calendars, college prep, and being there for my girls.
  • My weight loss. My own numbers and what I'm doing. Those are my results, not a prediction or a promise for anyone else.
  • AI that keeps the lights on. Including the Grok Bot setups I use to stay on track.

What this isn't

  • It isn't medical, mental-health or nutrition advice. I share what I do; talk to a qualified professional about yours.
  • It isn't a place for medication or prescription talk. That's between you and your doctor.
  • It isn't a guru thing. No "hack your brain," no "cure," no before-and-after miracles.

How I keep it honest

  • If I haven't tried a tool on camera yet, the page says so.
  • Prices come from the tool's official site, with the date I checked.
  • If I ever earn money from a link, it says "Paid link" right next to it. See the affiliate disclosure.
