What we collect, and what we don't.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Agent Radar is run by Dan, a sole proprietor in Florida, USA. We collect as little as possible. You can browse the directory and the Prompt Vault without an account and without giving us any personal information.

What we collect

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, we collect your email address. That's the only thing the signup form asks for.
  • Sponsored listings: if you buy a featured listing, we receive your name, email and the details you send us about your tool. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we keep the message so we can reply.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider, Vercel, automatically processes standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Analytics: if enabled, we use Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless and gives us aggregate stats (such as page views and referrers) without identifying individual visitors.

We don't use advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your email only to send the Agent Radar newsletter and related messages (like the welcome email). We use sponsor details only to set up, run and bill the featured listing and to keep business records. Technical data is used to run, secure and improve the site.

Who processes it

We rely on a few service providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Vercel: website hosting, server logs and (if enabled) Web Analytics
  • Resend: storing the newsletter list and sending emails
  • Stripe: processing payments for featured listings

We don't sell your data

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers above, or when required by law.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link. Click it and you won't get any more newsletters. To have your email address deleted completely, or to ask what we hold about you, email agentradar.dan@gmail.com and we'll handle it promptly. We keep payment and business records as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Children

Agent Radar is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us their email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the “Last updated” date above. If a change is significant, we'll mention it in the newsletter.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: agentradar.dan@gmail.com.
