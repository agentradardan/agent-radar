Refund Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy covers paid featured (sponsored) listings. Everything else on Agent Radar, including the directory, Prompt Vault and newsletter, is free.

Before your listing goes live

If you change your mind before your featured listing goes live, you'll get a full refund. Just email us.

After your listing goes live

Once the listing is live, the payment is non-refundable, with one exception: if we remove your listing early for reasons not caused by you (for example, we stop offering featured listings), we may give a pro-rated refund for the unused days, at our discretion.

If we remove a listing because it breaks our Terms (for example, it is illegal or misleading), no refund is due.

No auto-renewal

Featured listings are a one-time payment for a fixed period. Nothing renews or charges you again automatically. If you want another period, you buy it separately.

How to request a refund

Email agentradar.dan@gmail.com from the address you used to buy the listing, with your tool's name and the date you paid. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Your bank may take several business days to show it.
