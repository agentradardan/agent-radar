Agent Radar is an independent directory run by one person. Here's how to read what you find on it.

Editorial independence

We decide which tools to list and what to say about them. Tool makers can't pay to change their pricing summary, free-tier badge or description, and sponsorship doesn't buy a review or a better ranking outside the clearly labeled featured spot.

Opinions are opinions

Any verdicts, recommendations or comparisons on the site are our honest opinions, based on our own research at the time, and are labeled as such. They aren't statements of fact about a product, and your experience may differ.

Information changes

Pricing, plans, free tiers and features of AI tools change often, sometimes weekly. Each tool page shows when it was last checked. Always verify details with the vendor on its official website before you sign up, buy or make a decision.

Affiliate links and sponsored listings

Some links are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Some listings are paid placements and always carry a “Sponsored” label. Neither affects the pricing or free-tier info we show. Full details: Disclosure.

Not professional advice

Nothing on Agent Radar, including the Prompt Vault and the newsletter, is legal, financial, tax, security or other professional advice. Review AI output and talk to a qualified professional before relying on it for important decisions.

Tool names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify the tools. Listing a tool doesn't mean its maker endorses or is affiliated with Agent Radar.
